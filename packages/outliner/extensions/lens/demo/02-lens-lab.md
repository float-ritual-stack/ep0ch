Lens lab [page::lens-lab]
Three notes, three lenses. Edit a row's chips, or press a cell, and the table redraws.

Open any note, put the cursor where the table belongs, then choose **Run lens** in the power bar. Set `query=type=job` and `columns=title,stage`, then Alt+Enter. The `lens::` line stays in that note, live and editable. In an open draft, save to keep the inserted line.
