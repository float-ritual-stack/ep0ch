Structure: start here
Three notes below to try it on. Select words in a note and press `a` then `x` to extract them; or from a shell, `ep0ch ext act structure extract --block <note id> --quote "<the words>"`. Sorts: `ep0ch ext act structure sort-blocks --block <note id>`.

- ((plan|Garden plan)) has a passage and a list item to extract.
- ((seeds|Seed list)) is a list to sort by name, or by price with `--arg by=price`.
- ((beds|Beds)) has children to sort by title, created or `bed-size`.
