Intake demo: prior art for new requests

A request filed under the demo inbox below sets off the rule note beside it: after a quiet moment, and gathered with any others that arrive within 30 seconds, Intake writes a "Prior art and related" note under each and asks @prior-art to fill it in. It searches this outline (and, as configured, other outlines and an archive) for the request's words and links what came before, with a line on likely duplicates.

Try it: add a child to the demo inbox such as `The hose reel jams again [type::request]`. A request marked `[triage::done]`, or one that has its prior art already, is skipped. The rule runs at most 10 times a day.
