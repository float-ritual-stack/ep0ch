A FIVE-MINUTE REHEARSAL
Hold two thoughts.
Then move one.
This is a fictional conversation about a listening library. Every passage is a real block in a separate, private ep0ch outline. There is no generated interpretation hiding behind the sound.
  1. Play the reading. Enable sound if you want it. Each voice has a stable pitch and stereo position. The playhead follows time; your selected passage stays put.
  2. Move an account. Drag “The old words still sound” beside an earlier passage. Or select it and change its beat. You moved a cue, not a note.
  3. Follow a crossing. Switch to Crossings. A line means an authored reference, never inferred agreement. Select a node to trace its sources.
  4. Hold a disagreement. Choose a passage, press “Hold beside,” then choose another. The listening desk keeps both in view.
  5. Change the evidence. Edit a source in the reader. Its cue stays frozen at the earlier revision until you explicitly take the new words. Capture the crossing to keep an annotated reading in the outline.
Space play / pause · ← → step a beat (cycle views while stopped) · 1 / 2 / 3 change view · Ctrl/⌘ Z undo a score edit. Audio stops when the tab is hidden. No flashes or animated backgrounds.
