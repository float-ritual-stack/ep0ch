 ▾ ## 3. The expedition is a score

 ▌» The expedition is a score
 ▌ A score is a chosen order through material that remains where it was. It can
 ▌ include contradictions, silences, and returns.
 ▌
 ▌ Save a route through these notes. Read it in a different order. Change one
 ▌ source and hear what the same route now carries.
 ▌
 ▌ The map is temporary. The source stays canonical. The trail becomes a
 ▌ document.

 ▾ ## 4. The beacon repeats a missing word

 ▌» The beacon repeats a missing word
 ▌ Every ninety seconds the carrier drops out for exactly one syllable. The
 ▌ engineers called this a defect. The keeper marked it room for an answer.
 ▌
 ▌ 00:00 carrier / 00:41 silence / 00:42 carrier
 ▌
 ▌ A repaired transmission would erase the only place a second voice can enter.
 ▌ Preserve the gap until we know whose it is.
