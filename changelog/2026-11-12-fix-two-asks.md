### Each ask's row says where its own answer is (PIE-1768)

- **Two asks, one tile, true rows:** the answer tile shows the ask the cursor or a click is on; that ask's row names its side, and the other's says `show ▸`, never `beside ▸` beside someone else's answer.
- **A draft brought back names its tile:** an answered ask's row reads `· answered · beside ▸` (or `below ▾`, `in a tab`) while its tile shows it.
- **Run:** nothing; no PROTOCOL, GRAMMAR or schema change.
