### A tile closed by its × repaints its room at once (PIE-1769)

- **The tile beside fills the room in the same frame:** a click on a tile's × used to leave its neighbour at its old size over blank rows until the next key (seconds), about one close in two with motion on. The click's release settled the close's motion, and nothing asked for the frame after.
- **Any key or click that lands mid-motion draws the settled layout:** Esc and the first key after an open or a move too, not only the ×.
- **Run:** nothing; no PROTOCOL, GRAMMAR or schema change.
