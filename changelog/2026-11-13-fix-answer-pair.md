### A reader and its answer move as one (PIE-1794, PIE-1792 round three)

- **`^W a` on a reader or its answer** sends both to the drawer (or out of it): the reader where it goes, its answer beside it. One `^W B` brings both back.
- **In a group holding just the two,** `^W a` on either sends the group whole.
- **No path closes an answer:** where the pair can't move together (the screen would be left blank, or the answer sits in another group), the move is refused with why and both stay. The waiver that let an emptying group's answer close is gone.
- **Run:** nothing; no PROTOCOL, GRAMMAR or schema change.
