### The outline is its change log: machines follow it, backups archive it (PIE-1751, ADR 0009)

- **Every outline keeps its change log in its own file:** each committed row change is a log entry, in whole transactions, written by triggers the schema makes for every table. Schema 12 (`0012-change-log.ts`) writes the first genesis. The host compacts the log hourly, keeping one generation back. An edit costs about 12 rows, 3 KB and a fifth more time.
- **Another machine's outline is followed, not copied:** the gateway, the web client and link reads serve `~/outline-mirrors/<machine>/<outline>.sqlite`, the home's log applied from the copy's cursor. A change on the laptop shows on float-2 within seconds while the link is up. One process serves a machine's copies and the others attach. A range that doesn't follow the copy is refused: another log rebuilds the copy, another schema leaves it stale and names the machine to update.
- **`ep0ch backup follow`** runs the followers on their own, through the `ep0ch-follow` unit, written when `EP0CH_MCP_MIRRORS` is in `backup.env`. float-box following pie is pie's off-machine copy, seconds behind. `ep0ch doctor` says where each follower is.
- **Backups are the log, archived:** every 15 minutes `ep0ch backup run` writes each outline's new entries as a segment and uploads the archive folder with restic. `ep0ch backup restore … --at` replays the archive up to that moment into a new outline. The monthly drill replays every archive. The relay carries the log to the hub and refuses one that differs from the hub's archive.
- **Gone:** Litestream, its guard and units, the mirror's snapshot retakes and generations, the `.restic` mirror folder, the backup job's mirror step, and the store's `busy_timeout` with the dispatcher's busy retry. Restic snapshots from before (one `.sqlite` each) aren't read by `restore`; `restic dump` still gives one by hand.
- **Run:** `ep0ch install --apply` on float-2 and the laptop, then on float-2 `systemctl --user disable --now litestream.service`; float-box's follower and the bucket's retirement are in the PR's steps.
