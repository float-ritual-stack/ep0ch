### A group keeps the room its tiles had, and the answer tile follows the ask by mouse (PIE-1771, PIE-1770)

- **A gathered group takes its tiles' room:** `^W G` on two 44-column tiles of a 44/44/44/45 desk makes an 88-column group with each tile 44 wide inside. Before, the group stayed at 44 and the edit inside was 9 columns wide.
- **`^W < > + -` on a group size it whole:** its tiles keep their shares, `>` again keeps growing it in resize mode, and the hint row says `^W > wider group`. To size a tile inside the group, `^W e` into it first.
- **The answer tile's title follows a click on `show ▸`:** it changes in the same frame as the answer. Before, it showed the old ask for seconds.
- **A new ask in the same words as a saved one takes the tile when it's answered:** its row says `beside ▸`, and the saved twin's says `show ▸`.
- **A quick second click on `show ▸` still shows the answer:** a double click there no longer counts as selecting a word. A double click on a word in the text still selects and copies it.
- **Run:** nothing; no PROTOCOL, GRAMMAR or schema change.
