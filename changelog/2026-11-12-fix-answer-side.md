### The ask's row names its answer tile's side past a pair (PIE-1768)

- **Four tiles and groups:** the side comes from where the reader and its answer tile are drawn, however their splits nest, so a third tile opened beside or a gather no longer turns `beside ▸` into `show ▸`.
- **A tile on no clear side** (hidden by a zoom) is named by its number, `in tile 2`, never `show ▸`.
- **A click on `show ▸` shows the answer in the tile,** instead of unfolding its callout's source in the edit; a click while the tile shows it folds it as before.
- **Run:** nothing; no PROTOCOL, GRAMMAR or schema change.
