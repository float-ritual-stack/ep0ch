### Two parity reds of a busy night were two door bugs the walk only saw when the box was slow (PIE-1814, PIE-1454)

- **Not a chord that lapsed:** ctrl+k opens the power bar and ctrl+u clears its line; nothing times out between them. The door's only timed two-key windows are a second ctrl+] within 1.5 s and resize's 4 s idle, and neither was in these reds.
- **The power bar (parity-showcase, `X  ctrl+k ctrl+u  ran bar.edit, whose keys don't name it`):** true every night. The bar's clear, erase-word and cut (ctrl+u, ctrl+w, ctrl+x, alt+x, cmd+x, or a rebind's) ran `bar.edit`, whose keys named none of them. The line's key table owns those keys, so `bar.edit` now runs as the table's key (`asBoundKey`): the run says which key pressed it, and a rebind names the new one.
- **The links tile (parity-desk, `desk: reader on a note  b  links by id left the screen different from the key`):** `b` on a note whose only links are its children. Their read is two asks, so on a busy box it landed after the backlinks' one: `links` answered with a header selected, the first child was selected when the children came, and nothing showed it in the preview. The read is now part of what `links` waits for, and a selection that moves onto a child is shown, as the authored links' is.
- **Why only on a busy night:** the walk checked a second key again on a state built afresh, and clearing an empty line changes nothing, so it let ctrl+u go unless something else moved between its two looks. On a busy box something does: after a minute of slow frames the status bar says `slow: N ms to settle`, and its number moves by itself.
- **The walk, three fixes:** a second key that ran the same unnamed actions in the walk (where they changed the screen) and on the fresh build is a finding whether or not the fresh screen changes: `X` is red on the unfixed door every run, in 55 s, with no load. Its look leaves the slow chip out, and stands the clock and the uptime in where the bar reads them (they are two coloured items now, and the words the look used to replace were no longer side by side, so a minute's tick was a change).
- **A busy box on demand:** `PARITY_BUSY_MS=120` draws each probed key's frame that late. `PARITY_ONLY=X` went red on it before the fix (a click blamed for `slow: 158 ms` → `slow: 160 ms`). Three busy loops beside the run found the showcase's red in none of 2 runs and parity-desk's in 2 of 5.
- **PIE-1454** (`^W` then ctrl+k "ended ^W with no action", once, Oct 30): ^W then ctrl+k is a cancel and runs nothing by design; that night's fresh build differed by the daily layout's editor tile, fixed in PIE-1811. "desk: daily" is green 3 of 3 with frames drawn late.
- **Seen, not fixed:** `X` walks in 55 s of its 60 s budget on float-box with nothing else running, before and after this change; beside another test run it goes over by name (box-test gives it 180 s).
- **Run:** nothing; no PROTOCOL, GRAMMAR or schema change. The budget file is untouched.
