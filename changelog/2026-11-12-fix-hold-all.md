### Your own shell command and a restored ticket never stale the edit you have open (PIE-1760)

- **Whoever writes it:** a `properties.patch` on a note a door holds a draft of goes through the hold for the person too, not only agents. Your own `ep0ch work stage <id> doing` from another shell is saved and shows in the edit you have open, even while you type on the title line beside its chips, and ^S saves once.
- **A restore is one write:** work-later's "Back from later" line is now the patch's new `line` operation, so the stage and the dated line are saved together and land in your edit. The line comes in below your cursor while you type at the end.
- **A ticket that can't take the write waits:** when the door refuses (an agent's write on the line you're typing on), the groom's report says `waits` for that ticket, nothing is written, and the run goes on. The next run tries again.
- **`--as` works on `ep0ch work stage` and `set`:** it records the write as that agent, as the help said.
- **Run:** `ep0ch install --apply` (host and door together: PROTOCOL bumps, since `properties.patch` takes a new `line` operation; no grammar or schema change).
