### An ask retyped after a delete is answered again, and a half-typed ask waits for you (PIE-1743)

- **Typed back, asked again:** an ask you delete (and save) and then write again gets a new answer, at once; before, it got none for 10 minutes and its line said the old "answered 2 min ago". An undo that brings the ask back with its answer still keeps that answer, with no new run.
- **A pause never sends half an ask:** an ask on the line your cursor is on waits until you leave the line or save, and says `asks once you leave the line or save`. Lines you aren't on keep the 1.5 s quiet.
- **The line tells the truth:** an answer you take out from under its ask leaves the line saying `@yo's answer was taken out · ask again for a new one`, never the answer that's gone.
- **Run:** `ep0ch install --apply` (the door now sends its cursor's line with a draft read; an older door keeps the old timing).
