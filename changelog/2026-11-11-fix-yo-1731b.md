### A deleted ask takes its answer on ^S, a no-answer survives a restart, the answer tile doesn't wait for nothing (PIE-1731)

- **A deleted ask takes its answer when you save from the door:** the door's ^S lands before it lets go of its draft, so the answer is now taken out from the saved note on that draft's release (or at once when the release came first); both ways an answer lands.
- **A no-answer stays one across a restart:** which answers were no answers is kept in the outline's own metadata, so a retype after a deploy asks again.
- **The answer tile closes when its ask is gone** from the saved note, instead of saying "Waiting for the answer…" and being saved into the desk.
- **An edit opened on an ask line** puts the cursor at the ask's end, so backspacing or typing works on the ask.
- **Run:** `ep0ch install --apply` (host and door together).
