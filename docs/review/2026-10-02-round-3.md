## What holds

The round did what it said. Checked and clean across the lenses:

- **One engine.** No tree is written outside `layout.ts`, `flow.ts` and `screen-layout.ts`: no `.kids`,
  `.weights` or `.tabs` assignment or splice elsewhere. Every desk layout change goes through `Desk.apply`
  (or `ask` and `commit`); the host layer is a layout on the same module (`hostLayer`, `applyLayout` in
  `dock.ts`).
- **No subclasses.** No `extends Desk` anywhere in `src`; no `spec.name === …` switch in the desk or the
  engine. The old modules are live: `delivery.ts` is the board's spec, `board.ts` its message types, `hub/*`
  the welcome, Waiting and pinned specs and kinds.
- **One dispatcher, one `tile=` grammar.** `App.act`, the control socket and `ep0ch open` all go through the
  dispatcher; screen key handlers `press` or run actions; only overlay-local cursors (the hub picker's `j k`,
  the steps cursor, logon) aren't actions. `touches` is required by the type, so every one of about 190 actions
  declares it.
- **One draft lifecycle, one mode stack.** The board's composer, comments and reader edits all open a
  `DraftSession`; reader key precedence lives only in `ModeStack`/`PRECEDENCE`, and the walk confirmed the
  picker over the panel, and Esc popping one mode at a time.
- **Attribution.** Every control-socket `act` and `open` goes through `agentActor`: always `kind: "agent"`,
  `ext:` ids refused, "an agent (id) · action" said first. Person-only actions are refused before any tile is
  read. Clipboard writes (OSC 52) are the person's only; copy-on-select is mouse-only.
- **The walk.** Restore after a restart was exact (layout name, focus, the lock, the float, the shut drawer).
  The board's keep-2 cap held through three `alt+⏎` opens. A stale save kept the draft and copied it to disk.
  The theme cycled calm → night → classic → calm and persisted; `alt+v` said why it stayed on cells under tmux.

## D. Performance and docs drift

### Performance: nothing reaches medium
`apply` copies the tree once per operation (no `structuredClone`, no JSON round trip); the recursive freeze runs
only under test. It is called from actions, never from `render` or `tick`. Painting is capped near 60 fps.

| tiles | focus | resize | refusal (a drag's ghost) | move | `rects()` |

| 50 | 0.10 ms | 0.01 ms | 0.06 ms | 0.05 ms | 0.13 ms |

The whole desk at 240x70: render 4.1 ms at 20 tiles, 11.6 ms at 200; a border or header drag 2–3 ms per move at
20–50 tiles. The board with 4 lanes of 60 cards: render 1.7 ms, a card drag 1.7 ms per move.

  1 ms, but disk I/O on the key path. Low. Deferred: could debounce as the drags do.
- **P3** Render cost is per tile drawn; it nears 16 ms only around 200 tiles. Low. No action.
